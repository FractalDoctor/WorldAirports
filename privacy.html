WORLD AIRPORTS — PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: February 2026

DATA COLLECTION

World Airports does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app does not require an account, registration, or sign-in of any kind.

NETWORK REQUESTS

World Airports does not make any network requests. All airport, runway, and frequency data is bundled within the app. The app works entirely offline.

ANALYTICS AND TRACKING

World Airports does not include any analytics frameworks, crash reporters, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking of any kind.

LOCATION DATA

World Airports does not request or access your device's location. The maps displayed within the app show airport locations using bundled coordinate data only.

LOCAL STORAGE

World Airports does not store any user data on your device beyond standard iOS application caching managed by the operating system.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

World Airports uses Apple MapKit to display maps within the app. Apple's use of any data related to map rendering is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

World Airports does not collect data from anyone, including children.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a revised date.

CONTACT

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on the World Airports GitHub repository.
